\subsection{C/S/C+S Band Comparison}
Day-8 extends the focused C-band PCS confirmation to C, S, and combined C+S scenarios using entropy-aware BMD-GMI/NGMI and achievable information rate (AIR). The strongest scenario is S, with $\nu=0.32$, 12 spans, and 2.0 dBm/channel. It gives a paired BMD-GMI gain of 0.0448$\pm$0.0191 bits/symbol and an AIR gain of 0.0057$\pm$0.0024 Tb/s/channel. The C+S case uses a simplified lumped inter-band penalty and is not claimed as full Raman-calibrated WDM validation.
