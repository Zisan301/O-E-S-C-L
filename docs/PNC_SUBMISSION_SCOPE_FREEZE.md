# PNC Submission Scope Freeze

## Frozen manuscript scope

The manuscript should be submitted, if submitted in the current research direction, as a calibrated surrogate framework for PCS trend evaluation in coherent optical link scenarios.

The strongest supported claim is C-band calibrated diagnostic consistency under the Day-12/Day-13 span-count setting, together with PCS trend/stability analysis and transparent limitation evidence from Day-14.

## Supported claims

The project may claim:

- reproducible calibrated-surrogate workflow;
- PCS trend-evaluation framework;
- repeated-seed and symbol-count stability checks;
- C-band external diagnostic comparison against GNPy;
- Day-13 offset-calibrated diagnostic agreement for the tested C-band span-count sweep;
- Day-14 broader starter stress test showing limited transfer of the fixed offset;
- transparent reporting of failed generated S-band GNPy cases.

## Claims that must not be made

The project must not claim:

- full C/S-band external validation;
- raw absolute GNPy validation;
- experimental validation;
- direct Manakov/NLSE physical simulation;
- universal GSNR prediction across launch power, baud rate, spacing, channel loading and band;
- validated S-band external-reference agreement.

## Day-14 decision

Day-14 should be reported as a limitation/stress-test result. It does not strengthen the external-validation claim. Instead, it prevents overclaiming by showing that the Day-13 fixed offset is not universally transferable.

## Submission positioning

Best current positioning:

A calibrated surrogate framework for probabilistic constellation shaping trend evaluation in coherent optical link scenarios.

## Reviewer-facing honesty

If a reviewer asks whether the model is a physical simulator, answer:

No. It is a calibrated surrogate workflow with controlled PCS trend analysis and external diagnostic comparison. Physical fidelity beyond the tested diagnostic setting is treated as a limitation and future work.
